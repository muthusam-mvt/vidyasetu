# Manuscript Digitization & Heritage Preservation

## How are Indian manuscripts digitized?
The process typically has four stages: (1) condition assessment and light
conservation of the fragile palm-leaf, birch-bark, or paper folio; (2)
high-resolution multispectral or standard photography of each folio under
controlled, low-heat lighting to avoid damage; (3) metadata cataloguing —
recording script, language, subject, and provenance, often aligned to
standards like Dublin Core or TEI (Text Encoding Initiative) for manuscripts;
and (4) publishing the images and metadata through a searchable digital
repository, sometimes paired with OCR or manual transcription for
full-text search. India's National Mission for Manuscripts has coordinated
large-scale cataloguing and imaging of manuscript collections held in
institutions, temples, and private family archives across the country.

## Conservation challenges
Palm-leaf manuscripts are vulnerable to humidity, insect damage, and brittle
fracture; birch-bark is prone to flaking. Digitization projects usually pair
imaging work with basic conservation — cleaning, humidity stabilization, and
protective housing — since a digital copy does not replace the need to keep
the physical original intact for future re-imaging with better technology.

## 3D capture of monuments
Photogrammetry (stitching many overlapping photographs into a 3D model) and
LiDAR laser scanning are used to create accurate digital twins of temples,
forts, and stepwells. These models support structural monitoring over time,
disaster-recovery documentation, and public virtual-tour experiences that can
reduce physical foot traffic on fragile sites.

## Oral and intangible heritage
Folk music, oral epics, and ritual recitation are preserved through
community-based audio/video field recording, followed by transcription and
translation. Because these traditions are living and performative, archiving
projects increasingly try to document variation across performers and
regions rather than fixing a single "correct" version.
